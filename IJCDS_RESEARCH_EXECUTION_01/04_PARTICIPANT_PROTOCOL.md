# PARTICIPANT PROTOCOL FOR INDEPENDENT RECEIPT QUERY STUDY (PHASE 4)

## 1. Study Status & Executive Decision
Under Human Intervention **HI-01 (Approved Option A, 2026-11-05)**, the project preserves the authoritative 400 human-authored query instances (written by the researcher and an affiliated evaluator) with full transparency regarding transaction-date disambiguation. This protocol document archives the approved ethical and procedural methodology for external participant recruitment if supplementary replication is conducted.

## 2. Target Profile & Safeguards
- Cohort: 2-5 independent participants.
- Target Volume: 100-150 queries (20-30 queries per participant).
- Task: Inspect legitimate public receipt images, formulate retrieval queries from natural memory without seeing OCR text or candidate model rankings.
- Consent & Ethics: Institutional review adherence, anonymized participant IDs, zero private financial uploads.
