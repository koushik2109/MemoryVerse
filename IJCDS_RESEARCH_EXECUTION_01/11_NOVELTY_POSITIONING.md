# NOVELTY POSITIONING & JOURNAL FIT
- Central Question: When do text proxies suffice, and when do native-visual representations become necessary?
- Intended Contribution: Empirical regime analysis mapping 6 operational regimes across 1,626 items, supported by an empirical decision guide (Table XIII) and a lightweight two-stage cascade.
- Scoped Claim: Novelty lies in the systematic empirical boundary delineation, not an invented algorithm.
