# Journal Recommendations for MemoryVerse Paper

**Paper:** *"When Do Text Proxies Suffice? A Regime Analysis of Sparse, Dense, and Native-Visual Retrieval Under Proxy Noise"*

---

## Paper Summary

This manuscript presents a text-proxy-grounded multi-stage, multi-hop Multimodal RAG architecture. It evaluates when text proxies (OCR transcripts, machine captions) suffice and when native-visual representations are indispensable. Key contributions include sub-word BM25 dominance on optical OCR, dense retrieval query formulation sensitivity, conformal cascade calibration, and independent multi-hop bridging verification.

**Keywords:** Multimodal RAG, Text Proxies, Proxy Noise, Dense-Sparse Regime Analysis, Cascade Ranking, Conformal Risk Control, Multi-Hop Reasoning.

## Strategic Decision Guide

### Scenario 1: Hard Deadline (Acceptance required before mid-January 2027)
- **Select:** **IJCDS (University of Bahrain)**
- **Key Reason:** Fast-track peer review (4–6 weeks) contractually timed for Volume 17, Issue 1 (January 2027). APC is $265. Scopus Q3 indexed.

### Scenario 2: Highest Academic Prestige with Quick Q1 Feedback (Zero Cost)
- **Select:** **IP&M (Elsevier)**
- **Key Reason:** SCIE Q1 (IF 8.6), $0 cost, 10/10 fit for RAG/retrieval, and initial review reports arrive in 4–6 weeks (by mid-December).

### Scenario 3: Specialized Domain Authority (Can wait 5–8 months)
- **For Document AI & OCR noise focus:** **IJDAR (Springer / IAPR)**
- **For Multimodal Retrieval & Vision focus:** **IJMIR (Springer / ACM SIGMM)**
