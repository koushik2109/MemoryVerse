## 2. Breakdown of the 110 Evaluated Cases

The 110 cases are divided into two distinct subsets:
1. **Personal Photo Albums ($N=60$):**
   - 20 queries on Album 1 (`L10-A1-001` to `020`)
   - 20 queries on Album 2 (`L10-A2-001` to `020`)
   - 20 queries on Album 3 (`L10-A3-001` to `020`)
   - *Example prompt:* `"Which image file(s), if any, are the ground-truth matches for the supplied album query 'light yellow patchwork plush pajamas selfie'?"`
   - *Model generation (`gpt-4o-mini`):* `"Found 1 matching photo in album: IMG_4350.JPG matching 'light yellow patchwork plush pajamas selfie'."`
   - *Note:* These 60 cases originate from the personal multimodal vault experiments (Section 6.C, which has been cut from this submission!).
2. **Multi-Hop RAG Queries ($N=50$):**
   - 50 queries (`AUDIT-001` to `AUDIT-050`) spanning visual, audio, and metadata reasoning.
   - *Example prompt:* `"sound recording of a rooster crowing in the morning"`
   - *Model generation (`gpt-4o-mini`):* `"The specific detail about a sound recording of a rooster crowing in the morning is not present in the provided context. However, it is known that roosters are known for their crowing."`

## 4. Ambiguity and Flaws in the Current Manuscript Text

1. **Misleading Phrasing:** The current text states:
   > *"Dual-reviewer human auditing across 110 cases confirmed high inter-rater agreement and calibration against automated \texttt{gpt-4o-mini} scoring: observed agreement of 95.5\% for faithfulness ($\kappa = 0.8716$)..."*
   This phrasing ambiguously implies that $\kappa = 0.8716$ measures agreement between the human raters and `gpt-4o-mini`. In reality:
   - $\kappa$ measures **human-to-human** inter-rater agreement between the two authors.
   - `gpt-4o-mini` was the **generator** of the answers being evaluated, and in separate automated scoring served as an automated judge (with Qwen2.5/Qwen3.6 used as an independent LLM judge).
2. **Composition of the 110 Cases:** 60 of the 110 cases come from personal photo albums (legacy Section 6.C). Since Section 6.C was excised from the submission, citing an audit consisting primarily of cut album cases to validate multi-hop retrieval in Section 6.B is an unacknowledged domain mismatch.
3. **Absence of End-to-End Generation Accuracy:**
   - The paper is an empirical analysis of **retrieval regimes** (sparse vs. dense vs. native visual under proxy noise).
   - It does not report standard QA generation accuracy (Exact Match, F1, ANLS) for downstream LLM generation.
   - This must be clearly scoped: the paper evaluates retrieval stage performance, while generation quality was sampled via a 110-case qualitative rubric audit.

## 5. Proposed Manuscript Fix

1. **Clarify what $\kappa$ measures in Section 6.B and Section 7.5:**
   Change:
   > *"Dual-reviewer human auditing across 110 cases confirmed high inter-rater agreement and calibration against automated \texttt{gpt-4o-mini} scoring: observed agreement of 95.5\% for faithfulness ($\kappa = 0.8716$), 96.4\% for relevance ($\kappa = 0.8926$), and 98.2\% for correctness ($\kappa = 0.9451$; mean $\kappa = 0.9031$ \cite{zheng2023judging})."*
   To:
   > *"Dual-reviewer human auditing across 110 generated RAG answers (evaluating answers produced by \texttt{gpt-4o-mini}) demonstrated high inter-rater agreement between two independent authors: observed agreement of 95.5\% for faithfulness (Cohen's $\kappa = 0.8716$), 96.4\% for relevance ($\kappa = 0.8926$), and 98.2\% for correctness ($\kappa = 0.9451$; overall mean $\kappa = 0.9031$)."*
2. **State explicitly in Limitations (Section 7) that the paper focuses on retrieval metrics, not generation accuracy:**
   Add:
   > *"While our evaluation rigorously profiles retrieval metrics (MRR, Hit@$k$, nDCG) and verifies generation quality qualitatively via human-audited faithfulness and correctness rubrics, standard automated generation accuracy (e.g., Exact Match or ANLS) is not evaluated across the full corpus."*
3. **Headline claim impacted?** No. This clarifies provenance, prevents a reviewer from assuming $\kappa$ was human-vs-LLM, and transparently scopes the generation evaluation.
