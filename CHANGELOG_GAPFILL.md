# Gap-Filling Manuscript Changelog (`CHANGELOG_GAPFILL.md`)

This changelog records all manuscript-affecting changes, parameter shifts, and proposed edits resulting from the 3-Tier gap-filling experiments for the paper:
*"When Do Text Proxies Suffice? A Regime Analysis of Sparse, Dense, and Native-Visual Retrieval Under Proxy Noise"* (IJCDS submission).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> In accordance with ground rules:
> - The manuscript source files (`ijcds_body.tex`, `main.tex`, etc.) are **NOT** edited directly.
> - All suggested diffs are produced in `proposed_edits/`.
> - Every logged change records `Old Value`, `New Value`, and `Source File / Provenance`.
